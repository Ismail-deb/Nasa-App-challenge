How we test

One unusual year is not a trend. For each region we take the monthly averages from NASA MERRA-2, GPM IMERG and GLDAS, remove the seasonal cycle, and measure the change per decade with Sen's slope. A Mann-Kendall test, adjusted for month-to-month persistence, says whether that change is statistically significant. "Not significant yet" does not mean "no trend".
